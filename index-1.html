02 / 13 · Executive Summary
4.1 Executive Summary

Built for
0.75 MAP

The BHS concept design is quantified for the 0.75 MAP build. Manual sorting is adopted at this scale, so Tilt Tray Sorters and Individual Carrier Systems are not used on a project of this size.

  • 1 Check-In Conveyor line, 12 units
  • 1 Delivery Line to HBS Area
  • 1 HBS Level 1 & Level 2 Security Screening Line
  • 1 Inclined Make-Up Carousel
  • 1 Horizontal Arrivals Reclaim Carousel

06 / 13 · Level Detail
4.4 HBS Operations

Level by level

01
EDS Machine
Automatic
Continuous conveyor speed of 0.5 m/s at 300 mm spacing, holding a consistent 20 bpm operational capacity. Cleared bags divert at the VSU onto the cleared line.
02
Operator
On-Screen Res.
2A gives 45s to a first decision; 2B extends review to 135s total. Operators typically clear 50–90% of referred bags in this window.
03
Dedicated
Workstation
Unconstrained review time, supplemented with offline ETD or a stand-alone x-ray machine. Cleared bags route back to the Make-Up Device.
04
PAX
Reconciliation
Suspected non-IED threat (e.g. dangerous goods): bag is physically removed from the BHS for manual investigation.
05
Threat
Bag
Local IED protocols engage: a Threat Containment Unit or equivalent explosive-mitigating measure, per local security services.
07 / 13 · Flow Diagram
4.2 HBS Levels

The 135-second
decision window

A bag entering the ECAC Std. 3 EDS is cleared automatically 70% of the time; the remaining 30% routes to an operator desk for On-Screen Resolution.

  • 45s first-look review window; up to 95% of that stream clears
  • +90s extended review at the second desk on the remaining 5%
  • Combined 135s travel time delivers a 95–97% overall clear rate
  • Un-cleared and no-decision bags proceed to Level 3, offline ETD review
  • Just 0.005% of bags ever reach reconciliation or threat handling

11 / 13 · Departure BHS
4.4 Description of BHS Design

Check-in
to make-up

12 check-in positions on a single row, each a 3-stage conveyor:

  • Weighing / Labelling: PAX hand-over point, integrated weigh scale, bag tag applied
  • Dispatch conveyor: interfaces with the take-away belt, angled to lay bags flat
  • Collector conveyor: feeds the ECAC Standard 3 screening line toward make-up

Cleared bags divert onto a lower-level line and are sorted onto the Inclined Make-Up Carousel by handheld scanner.

12 / 13 · Inbound BHS
4.4 Description of BHS Design

Arrivals
reclaim

A flat-plate carousel serves the PAX reclaim hall: compact, with a narrow turning radius, well suited to public space. Rubber slats keep it low-noise, low-maintenance, and free of pinch points.

  • Low-profile, lip-free surface, no lifting required to reclaim a bag
  • Fed by ground handlers directly, or an automated delivery system
  • 100 m presentation length on the reclaim device
  • Space reserved outside the carousel for later baggage security equipment
